# Raise coaching price to $3,500

Asked: I run a coaching business doing $30k a month with 15 active clients at $2,000 each. I want to raise the price to $3,500. Options: raise for everyone now, raise for new clients only, or keep $2,000. Close rate is about 50% on sales calls. I am leaning toward raising for everyone because I am underpaid.

The founder runs a coaching business doing $30k a month with 15 active clients at $2,000 each. Close rate on sales calls is about 50%. The proposal is to raise the price to $3,500. Three paths are on the table: raise for all 15 current clients now, raise for new clients only, or keep the price at $2,000. Success means more revenue per client without losing enough clients or closes to wipe out the gain. At $3,500, the same 15 clients would bring in $52,500 a month. If everyone is raised, the business stays ahead of today's $30k as long as at least 9 of 15 stay. The founder is leaning toward raising for everyone now, because they feel underpaid. The founder did not share capacity limits, contract terms, lead volume, or client results.

Options:
- A: Raise to $3,500 for everyone now
- B: Raise to $3,500 for new clients only, keep current clients at $2,000
- C: Keep the price at $2,000 for everyone

Assumptions:
- The $2,000 and $3,500 prices are per client per month.
- Current clients are not locked into contracts that block a price change.
- The founder has capacity limits but did not state them.
- The 50% close rate was measured at $2,000 and will likely drop at $3,500 by an unknown amount.
- Lead flow is steady enough to replace clients who leave.
