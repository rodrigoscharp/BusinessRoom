# Draft resolution (Chair, after Round 2)

**Recommendation:** GO WITH CONDITIONS on Option B. Decline the $4M seed. Raise the $1M from operator angels, sign only after the co-founders agree in writing, and spend it on an onboarding owner and then a closer, after shipping annual prepay.

**Sequence and conditions (the 21-day window starts today, October 9):**
1. **Days 1 to 7.** Offer annual prepay (10 months' price, two months free) to all 200 customers; setup fee for new customers only. Call every customer who churned in the last 90 days and write down why. Ask the five angels for written soft-commits (at least four of five by day 7). Tell the VC the answer is no.
2. **By day 7 (October 16).** Both co-founders spend one day out of the office and sign a one-page memo: Core Focus (shops under 10 technicians), the 18-month Accountability Chart with names in seats, and the use of funds. No investor signature before this memo.
3. **Day 14 decision rule.** If 60 or more customers have prepaid and both founders prefer it, decline both raises and run C (the Buffett and Martell path). Otherwise sign B by day 21.
4. **Use of funds (B).** First hire: an onboarding owner within 30 days, who owns logo churn on the weekly Scorecard. Second: a closer by day 60, trained on at least 20 recorded calls. An assistant paid from profit. No paid media until monthly churn is under 2%.
5. **Checkpoints.** Day 90: monthly churn under 2% and the CEO under 50 hours a week (15-minute time audit). Day 120: the closer's fully loaded CAC under $1,100. Six months: churn under 1.5% and the CEO on fewer than half of sales calls.

**Cheapest test:** the prepay offer and the churn calls, both done before any signature.
**Kill criteria:** if the founders cannot sign one memo by day 7, take no money and run C; if churn is not under 2% by day 90, freeze the closer hire and fix onboarding first.
