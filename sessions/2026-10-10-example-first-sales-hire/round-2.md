## Paul Graham (weight 2)

SEAT: GRAHAM
I DISAGREE MOST WITH: Hormozi. Their argument: hand off the average leads, write the script, pay on cash collected. My rebuttal: the average leads are where you learn why growth is 5%. Those are the people who didn't buy. A closer with no playbook gets half your close rate and you can't tell if the leads or the closer are bad. That is noise, not learning. "You have to do sales yourself initially." (S096)

I CONCEDE: Buffett's and Martell's best point is that a commission-only closer costs a few thousand dollars a month at most, is capped, and is reversible, while a tired solo founder sells worse. That changes my sequencing. I was treating filtering leads as enough relief. It probably isn't, and a cheap trial is not a risk worth refusing once I have the data.

ON THE CRUXES:
1. Demand problem first. 1.2% a week is a sign you haven't figured it out yet (S100), and the answer is in the calls, especially the lost ones. Capacity may become the issue, but nobody has shown me unanswered leads.
2. Not before a playbook. A limited handoff works only after the founder can say why deals close and die. Record the calls first, and the trial follows from that.

STANCE: Conditional yes on C for 30 days with calls recorded and filtered, then a commission-only closer trial on average leads. No full-time AE.
