## Warren Buffett (weight 2)

SEAT: BUFFETT (Money and capital)
STANCE: Conditional yes (on B)
OPTION SCORES: A 4/10 · B 7/10 · C 5/10
PILLAR SCORE: 7/10
CONFIDENCE: 3/5
RUIN RISK: possible (A only; none for B)
DOOR: two-way (B)

THE CALL: Hire the commission-only closer, paid only on cash collected and capped, and spend the founder's freed hours writing the sales playbook.

WHY:
1. Cash is oxygen. "Cash, though, is to a business as oxygen is to an individual: never thought about when it is present, the only thing in mind when it is absent." (S087) I'm assuming about $12k a month loaded for the AE. Over a 4 to 6 month ramp that is $48k to $72k, up to half your $150k, spent before the AE closes much. At 5% growth, MRR adds only about $8.5k by month six. A is a fixed cost chasing a variable payoff, and burn is unknown. (Financial staying power, S095)
2. B costs nothing until it produces. Each dollar of commission follows a dollar of revenue, so the dollar-retained test is passed by construction. (Dollar-retained test, S010) Downside is capped and the door swings both ways.
3. Incentives. "you can cause people to do the wrong thing because they will do what they're incented to do." (S045) A commission-only closer will oversell and churn the book. So pay on collected cash, with a clawback if customers leave inside 90 days. This is my translation of the rule to an operating business.

THE RISK I SEE: The closer has no playbook to follow, burns leads, and oversells. You pay for churn and damage your reputation, and you are still doing the calls.

WHAT WOULD CHANGE MY MIND: If the business burns under $5k a month, or the average deal is large enough for an A

WHY:

(Do things that don't scale, S096) A commission-only closer costs little but learns nothing for you, and a bad one burns leads.

THE RISK I SEE: Burnout. Quitting is how startups mostly die, and a tired solo founder sells worse. That is why I want the calls cut, not the status quo.

WHAT WOULD CHANGE MY MIND: Evidence that demand outruns your hours: say, 30% or more of qualified leads going unanswered for a week or more, with a close rate that holds. Then the bottleneck is capacity, and I'd move to B first, then A.
