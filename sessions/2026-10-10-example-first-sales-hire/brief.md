# First sales hire or keep selling

Asked: I'm a solo founder of a B2B SaaS at $25k MRR growing 5% a month. I do every sales call myself (about 20 hours a week) and I'm burning out. Should I hire a full-time account executive at $120k base, hire a part-time commission-only closer, or keep selling myself for six more months? I have $150k in the bank.

Solo founder of a B2B SaaS. Revenue is $25k MRR, growing 5% a month. The founder runs every sales call, about 20 hours a week, and says they are burning out. Cash in the bank is $150k. Three paths are on the table: a full-time account executive at $120k base, a part-time commission-only closer, or keep selling personally for six more months. Success means sales keep growing at or above the current rate while the founder gets time and energy back, without putting the company's cash at risk. The founder did not state a preference. The burnout signals they want relief soon, but they listed staying the course as a real option.

Options:
- A: Hire a full-time account executive at $120k base
- B: Hire a part-time commission-only closer
- C: Keep selling personally for six more months

Assumptions:
- Profit margin and monthly burn are unknown, so true runway is unknown.
- The $120k base works out to about $10k a month before benefits, commission, and taxes, roughly 40% of current MRR.
- A new AE will likely take 3 to 6 months to ramp to full productivity.
- Deal size, sales cycle length, and close rate are unknown.
- The sales process is assumed to live mostly in the founder's head, not in a written playbook.
- Commission-only closers are assumed to earn a percentage of first-year contract value, with no base.
