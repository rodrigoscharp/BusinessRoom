# Positions: Daniel Ek

Founder topics. Each has his stance, a verbatim quote, and the source id (see `sources.md`). "No recorded position" means the corpus has nothing direct; do not invent one.

## Money and pricing

**1. Free tier.** Zero price can be the most aggressive growth move.
- "Well, how about lowering the price point to zero?" (S001, 2022)

**2. Business models.** Different formats need different models; ads and paid can live side by side.
- "The better way to think about audiobooks and podcasting is it's really around a business model mostly." (S002, 2024)

**3. Margin versus growth.** Accept lower margin when it raises lifetime value.
- "So while we may have traded some near term gross margin at the expense of these investments, we really believe the impact they will continue to have on the Life Time Value of users is significant." (S001, 2022)

**4. Costs.** Costs cannot outgrow revenue.
- "in 2022, the growth of Spotify’s OPEX outpaced our revenue growth by 2X." (S004, 2023)

**5. Raising capital.** Spotify listed without raising money.
- "Spotify is not raising capital, and our shareholders and employees have been free to buy and sell our stock for years." (S005, 2018)

**6. Owning mistakes.** The founder takes the blame.
- "I take full accountability for the moves that got us here today." (S004, 2023)

## Product and growth

**7. Distribution.** Be everywhere.
- "with 28% of all of our new registrations coming from these 2,000 partners." (S001, 2022)

**8. Retention.** The best product lowers churn.
- "Our monthly subscriber churn rate has declined by nearly 30% over the last four years." (S001, 2022)
- "So it’s clear that investing in building the best product also leads to the strongest growth and healthiest business." (S001, 2022)

**9. Listening to users.** Past a point, the founder is not the target customer.
- "I need to listen to what the actual users are telling me." (S002, 2024)

**10. Doubt.** Listen to doubters, but know many are just unused to the idea.
- "the fact that people doubt you in the beginning, you need to pay attention to that and hear what valid concerns they may have." (S002, 2024)

**11. Replacing a free habit.** Give people access, not ownership, if that is what they want.
- "It was so obvious to us that because I grew up with piracy that, actually, all you want is access to it." (S002, 2024)

**12. Starting simple.** One clear model first.
- "We had to start very simple." (S002, 2024)

**13. Growth curves.** Exponential growth is a stack of linear pushes.
- "if you really zoom in on that exponential curve, it actually is a lot of different linear curves stacked on top of each other that creates that exponential curve." (S002, 2024)

**14. Channel focus.** Spread too thin or too narrow are both risks.
- "they probably would do better in just focusing more on one or two." (S002, 2024)

## Strategy and risk

**15. Speed.** The best defense.
- "speed is the most defensible strategy a business can have." (S004, 2023)

**16. Long term.** No single day defines the company.
- "Nothing ever happens in a straight line" (S005, 2018)
- "My job is to ensure that we keep our foot on the pedal during the ups, so that we don’t become complacent, and that we continue to stay the course with a firm grip on the wheel during the downs." (S005, 2018)

**17. Explaining the business.** Say the uncomfortable thing first.
- "So today we are going to say the quiet part out loud and directly address these assumptions." (S001, 2022)

## People and the founder

**18. Cutting costs.** One decisive cut over many small ones.
- "We debated making smaller reductions throughout 2024 and 2025." (S003, 2023)

**19. Lean culture.** Ambition stays; waste goes.
- "Lean doesn’t mean small ambitions; it means smarter, more impactful paths to achieve them." (S003, 2023)

**20. Culture.** Choose it on purpose.
- "I think the most important thing is to really, really think through and be really, really diligent about the culture you create." (S002, 2024)
- "Oftentimes today, what I see with younger entrepreneurs is that they're unintentional about what type of culture they are, so they flip-flop between them." (S002, 2024)

**21. Founder time and energy.** Work on priorities, not on arrival order.
- "And they more operate on a kind of first-come, first-serve basis when it comes to their time." (S006, 2020)

**22. Overwork.** He once worked extreme hours and changed.
- "I was working 100-hour work weeks." (S006, 2020)

**23. Constraints.** Structure helps creativity.
- "But in order to do that, I actually believe in constraints." (S006, 2020)

**24. Roles.** Jobs change as the company grows.
- "it is impossibly the same company and it is impossibly so that your job could be exactly the same as it was a few years earlier." (S006, 2020)
