# Principles: Jensen Huang

12 decision rules. Confidence: high = 3 or more sources, medium = 2, low = 1.

1. **Choose work by its importance, not by today's market.**
   "Nobody has to do a business case on it." (S001) / "At the time, this was a zero billion dollar market." (S002)
   Sources: S001, S002, S005. Confidence: high.

2. **Create the market and the technology at the same time.**
   "Almost everything we do, we create technology, we create markets." (S001)
   Sources: S001, S002. Confidence: medium.

3. **Decide what to give up; retreat when you cannot win.**
   "By leaving the phone market, we opened our minds to invent a new one." (S002)
   Sources: S002. Confidence: low.

4. **Admit a wrong strategy early, and ask for help.**
   "you will have humility to confront failure, admit mistake, and ask for help." (S002)
   Sources: S002, S007. Confidence: medium.

5. **Endurance and character beat raw intelligence.**
   "My ability to endure pain and suffering, my ability to work on something for a very, very long period of time, my ability to handle setbacks and see the opportunity just around the corner I consider to be my superpowers" (S007)
   Sources: S007, S006, S002. Confidence: high.

6. **Setbacks are where the next opportunity is.**
   "One setback after another, we shook it off and skated to the next opportunity." (S007)
   Sources: S007, S002. Confidence: medium.

7. **When you get one try, design the process so it works the first time.**
   "So let’s make it perfect. We get one shot." (S005)
   Sources: S005. Confidence: low.

8. **Build the base before the platform can pay.**
   "And a large CUDA install base needed customers buying new applications." (S002)
   Sources: S002, S005, S004. Confidence: high.

9. **Speed is quality.**
   "Speed is performance." (S005)
   Sources: S005, S002. Confidence: medium.

10. **Leaders exist to serve and empower the people doing the work.**
   "Those are the reasons why the management team exists, in service of all of the other people that work at the company" (S001)
   Sources: S001, S006. Confidence: medium.

11. **Founders must believe it is easier than it is.**
   "You have to get yourself to believe that it’s not that hard, because it’s way harder than you think." (S005)
   Sources: S005, S001. Confidence: medium.

12. **Find a craft and make it your life's work.**
   "I hope you find a craft." (S007)
   Sources: S007, S002. Confidence: medium.
