# Positions: Jensen Huang

Founder topics. Each has his stance, a verbatim quote, and the source id (see `sources.md`). "No recorded position" means the corpus has nothing direct; do not invent one.

## Money and pricing

**1. Pricing a better product.** Go above where competitors dare.
- "We also chose a cost point that is substantially higher than the highest price that we think that any of our competitors would be willing to go." (S005, 2023)

**2. Business cases.** Importance first; the spreadsheet comes later.
- "Nobody has to show me a financial forecast." (S001, 2024)

**3. Funding a long bet.** A core business pays while the new market forms, and profits can drop for years.
- "Nvidia's profits took a huge hit." (S002, 2023)

**4. Runway.** He started with very little cushion.
- "And Laurie and I only had about six months in the bank." (S001, 2024)

**5. Stock price and doubt.** Expect to be embarrassed.
- "It’s like when your stock price gets hit, it’s embarrassing no matter how you think about it." (S005, 2023)

## Product and markets

**6. Market size.** Ignore today's size if the work matters.
- "We find inspiration, not from the size of a market, but from the importance of the work, because the importance of the work is the early indicators of a future market." (S001, 2024)

**7. Market creation.** Build technology and market together.
- "All these different areas we’re trying to create the market while we’re creating the technology." (S001, 2024)

**8. Platforms.** Build layers others stand on.
- "A platform is something that you build other things upon." (S004, 2025)

**9. Moats.** The ecosystem is the moat.
- "That network is essentially your moat." (S005, 2023)

**10. Speed.** Faster is better.
- "Time to market is performance." (S005, 2023)

**11. Getting there first.** Being early gives time to build the ecosystem.
- "For a long time, nobody paid me any nevermind, so I had lots and lots of time to build up all these ecosystems and applications and relationships and ecosystems" (S004, 2025)

**12. Unique contribution.** Do what only you can do.
- "Live a life of purpose, to do something that nobody else in the world would do or can do" (S001, 2024)

## Strategy and risk

**13. Retreat.** Leaving a market can be the winning move.
- "Our strategic retreat paid off." (S002, 2023)

**14. Admitting mistakes.** Tell partners early.
- "I was embarrassed to ask." (S002, 2023)

**15. Competition.** In crowded markets, find the right way, fast.
- "We now have 89 competitors." (S001, 2024)

**16. Urgency.** Always run.
- "Run, don't walk." (S002, 2023)

**17. Luck.** He credits vision for putting Nvidia where luck could strike.
- "That was luck founded by vision." (S003, 2024)

## People and the founder

**18. Org design.** Flat, with many direct reports.
- "I have 60 direct reports." (S006, 2024)

**19. Information and power.** Share it.
- "I don’t believe in a culture, in an environment where the information that you possess is the reason why you have power." (S001, 2024)

**20. Teaching.** Show the reasoning.
- "So, I show people how to reason through things all the time" (S001, 2024)

**21. Character.** Talent matters less than character.
- "It's a hundred percent character." (S006, 2024)

**22. Pain.** It is part of the journey, and he would not wish the full dose on anyone.
- "And so I think athletes learn that pain and suffering is part of the journey." (S006, 2024)
- "Nobody in their right mind would do it." (S005, 2023)

**23. Founder belief.** Necessary to survive the doubt.
- "You have to really, really believe it and really, really want it." (S005, 2023)

**24. Hard things.** They should be hard.
- "If you want to do extraordinary things, it shouldn’t be easy." (S003, 2024)
