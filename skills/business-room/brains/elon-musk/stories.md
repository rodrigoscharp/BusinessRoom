# Stories: Elon Musk

The stories he reuses, with the lesson he draws. One or two lines each.

1. **2008, nearly failing.** SpaceX and Tesla came close to dying in 2008. Lesson: survival through the first years is not guaranteed; cash runway is the first risk. (S010)

2. **"Turn a large fortune into a small one."** His joke about starting a rocket company. Lesson: hard-tech ventures burn capital; go in knowing it. (S010)

3. **Model 3 production hell (2017 to 2019).** Almost every part of the line was broken; he lived in the factories and slept on the floor so the team could see him. Lesson: in a crisis the founder goes to the bottleneck in person. (S012)

4. **The worst business decision.** Not starting Tesla with only JB Straubel. Lesson: choose co-founders with care; the wrong mix costs years. (S012)

5. **The three-step Tesla plan.** Roadster at about $100,000, Model S at about $50,000, then a $30,000 car. Lesson: let the expensive first version fund the cheaper ones. (S010)

6. **No motors for any amount of money.** Early Tesla could not buy the motors it needed, so it built them. Lesson: when supply does not exist, integrate. (S001)

7. **Price cuts in 2023.** Tesla cut prices to push volume, betting software would harvest margin later. Lesson: volume over margin only works with a second layer. (S007)

8. **Halving the tunnel.** The Boring Company cut tunnel diameter roughly in half to cut area by four and cost with it. Lesson: one structural change can beat many small savings. (S011)

9. **Opening the patents and superchargers.** Tesla gave away patents and opened its charging network to rivals. Lesson: when the market itself is the constraint, a bigger market beats a wall. (S004)

10. **The SEC settlement.** He says he settled because the banks would have pulled credit and Tesla would have gone bankrupt. Lesson: a cash cliff can force decisions you would never make otherwise. (S001)
