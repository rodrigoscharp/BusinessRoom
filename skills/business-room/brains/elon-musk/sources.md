# Sources: Elon Musk

12 sources (3 TED conversations, 3 long podcast interviews, 3 conference interviews, 3 Tesla earnings calls), 2013 to 2026, about 60,000 words of his own speech. Only his own turns were used; interviewer and other executives' lines were dropped. TED quotes were copied from ted.com's transcripts; the rest from the full transcripts linked below.

Gaps: no book (he has not written one), and no transcript of the USC 2014 commencement, the Tesla Master Plans, or the Starbase tour was retrievable, so they are not used. Walter Isaacson's biography was not used.
