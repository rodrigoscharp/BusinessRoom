# Positions: Mark Zuckerberg

Founder topics. Each has his stance, a verbatim quote, and the source id (see `sources.md`). "No recorded position" means the corpus has nothing direct; do not invent one.

## Money and pricing

**1. Profit and mission.** Profit funds the product.
- "Simply put: we don’t build services to make money; we make money to build better services." (S005, 2012)

**2. Profitability as a cushion.** Get profitable to survive uncertain years and keep investing.
- "a big part of why I wanted to improve our profitability is to give ourselves the ability to go through what is a somewhat unpredictable and volatile period over the next 5 or 10 years." (S008, 2024)

**3. Cost discipline.** Small efficiency gains at scale are worth billions.
- "So if we can do that 10% more efficiently, we're saving billions or tens of billions of dollars." (S002, 2024)

**4. Monetizing a new product.** Grow use first, then fix revenue per unit.
- "Currently, the monetization efficiency of Reels is much less than feed, so the more that Reels grows, even though it adds engagement to the system overall, it takes some time away from feed and we actually lose money." (S007, 2023)

**5. Selling the company.** He turned down a sale because he would only build the same thing again.
- "So I think if I sold this company, I'd just go build another company like this and I kind of like the one I have." (S002, 2024)

## Product and growth

**6. Shipping.** Done beats perfect.
- "We have the words “Done is better than perfect” painted on our walls to remind ourselves to always keep shipping." (S005, 2012)

**7. Testing.** Run many versions at once and let the data decide.
- "At any given point in time, there isn't just one version of Facebook running, there are probably 10,000." (S006, 2018)

**8. Users.** Assume they are right about what they value.
- "One of my core guiding principles in designing products is that people are smart." (S003, 2025)
- "But if you think something someone is doing is bad and they think it's really valuable, most of the time in my experience, they're right and you're wrong." (S003, 2025)

**9. Expansion.** Go where requests are highest.
- "So we just ranked schools by how many requests we had for it, and then whenever we had enough server capacity, we would just launch at the school that had the most requests to us about wanting it." (S006, 2018)

**10. Network effects.** A platform comes alive once enough people use it.
- "And it’s only when you have millions of people using something that you start getting the whole developer community really starting to experiment and build stuff because now there are going to be people who actually use it." (S001, 2023)

**11. Getting started.** Clarity comes from building.
- "Ideas don’t come out fully formed. They only become clear as you work on them. You just have to get started." (S004, 2017)
- "The idea of a single eureka moment is a dangerous lie." (S004, 2017)

## Strategy and risk

**12. Speed versus safety.** Not moving is riskier.
- "We have another saying: “The riskiest thing is to take no risks.”" (S005, 2012)
- "The idea is that if you never break anything, you’re probably not moving fast enough." (S005, 2012)

**13. Fear.** He fears missing the opportunity more than failing.
- "I have more fear in my life that we aren't going to maximize the opportunity that we have than that we mess something up and the business goes badly." (S006, 2018)

**14. Long bets.** Conviction first; the spreadsheet comes later.
- "I think a lot of the biggest bets that people make are often just based on conviction and values." (S002, 2024)

**15. Platform dependence.** Build your own layer.
- "I don't want any of those other companies telling us what we can build." (S002, 2024)

**16. Open source.** Open the infrastructure, keep the product.
- "We take a lot of the low-level infrastructure and we make that open source." (S002, 2024)

**17. Being misunderstood.** Expect it.
- "Anyone working on a big vision will get called crazy, even if you end up right." (S004, 2017)

## People and the founder

**18. The founder's job.** Set direction, then recruit.
- "A lot of what I spend my time on is trying to get awesome people onto the teams." (S003, 2025)

**19. Org design.** Fewer layers, faster decisions.
- "we're working on flattening our org structure and removing some layers of middle management to make decisions faster" (S007, 2023)

**20. Hands-on managers.** New engineers, managers included, go through the same engineering bootcamp.
- "There are a lot of folks in the industry who manage engineers and don’t want to code themselves" (S005, 2012). He wants hands-on managers instead.

**21. Merit over politics.** The best idea should win, not the loudest person (S005, 2012). See frameworks.md, The Hacker Way.

**22. Drive.** He needs to be building.
- "I just feel constitutionally that I'm doing something wrong if I'm not building something new." (S002, 2024)

**23. Purpose.** People need to feel part of something bigger.
- "Purpose is that sense that we are part of something bigger than ourselves, that we are needed, that we have something better ahead to work for." (S004, 2017)
