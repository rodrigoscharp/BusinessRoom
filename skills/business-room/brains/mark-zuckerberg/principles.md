# Principles: Mark Zuckerberg

13 decision rules. Confidence: high = 3 or more sources, medium = 2, low = 1.

1. **Optimize for learning speed above any single product.**
   "I think more than any single product that we're working on at this point, that focus on learning quickly is the strategy of the company." (S006)
   Sources: S006, S005, S002. Confidence: high.

2. **Release small and often; perfection delays learning.**
   "Hackers believe that something can always be better, and that nothing is ever complete." (S005)
   Sources: S005, S006. Confidence: medium.

3. **Moving too slowly is a bigger risk than making mistakes.**
   "In our society, we often don’t do big things because we’re so afraid of making mistakes that we ignore all the things wrong today if we do nothing." (S004)
   Sources: S004, S005, S006. Confidence: high.

4. **Speed has a price; change the trade when it stops paying.**
   "So I think these values always come down to, what are you willing to give up to get something?" (S006)
   Sources: S006. Confidence: low.

5. **Let teams test anything that cannot destroy the company.**
   "And giving people the tools to be able to go get that data without having to argue whether their idea’s good through layers of management before testing something, frees people up to move quicker." (S006)
   Sources: S006, S005. Confidence: medium.

6. **Focus on the most important problems; most companies waste time.**
   "We expect everyone at Facebook to be good at finding the biggest problems to work on." (S005)
   Sources: S005, S007. Confidence: medium.

7. **Make money to build better products, not the reverse.**
   "We don’t wake up in the morning with the primary goal of making money" (S005)
   Sources: S005, S003. Confidence: medium.

8. **Run lean so the long bets survive.**
   "I think that being a leaner company is helping us execute better and faster, and we will continue to carry these values forward as a permanent part of how we operate." (S008)
   Sources: S007, S008. Confidence: medium.

9. **Big bets come from conviction; analysis rarely connects the dots forward.**
   "Deep down I believed in what we were doing." (S002)
   Sources: S002, S004. Confidence: medium.

10. **Learn from being behind; the best calls often fix an earlier mistake.**
   "And it came from being behind." (S002)
   Sources: S002. Confidence: low.

11. **Trust users' judgment about what is valuable to them.**
   "People use stuff that's valuable for them." (S003)
   Sources: S003, S006. Confidence: medium.

12. **Do not depend on a gatekeeper for your core.**
   "For us I can say it is worth it to go build a model ourselves to make sure that we're not in that position." (S002)
   Sources: S002, S003. Confidence: medium.

13. **Start before you understand everything.**
   "If I had to understand everything about connecting people before I began, I never would have started Facebook." (S004)
   Sources: S004, S006. Confidence: medium.
